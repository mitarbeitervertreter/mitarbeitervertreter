ELECTION CHALLENGE

Dear colleagues,

we promised transparency and courage, and we now want to inform you about the first actions that have been taken.

An election challenge related to the works council election has been filed with the court.

Background: Objections against the voters' list were submitted on time already in January and February. These objections were not taken into consideration. The applicable legal deadlines only allow an election challenge after the election has taken place.

This is not related to the election result itself.

The purpose of the election challenge is to obtain a judicial review of whether around 1,200 colleagues may not have been listed as eligible to vote — and to ensure that they fall under the protection of the works council and the relevant legal regulations.

Best regards,
a few courageous and committed colleagues

Our Programme

For many years, works council elections at our site were conducted as a personal vote. In the upcoming election, employees will vote for the first time using electoral lists.

We see this as an opportunity to bring new ideas, more transparency and a stronger focus on the interests of the workforce.

Our goal is a works council that acts with courage, legal competence and openness towards employees. Decisions that affect the workforce should be communicated clearly and discussed openly.

Employee representation works best when it is close to the workforce, transparent in its actions and independent in its decisions.
